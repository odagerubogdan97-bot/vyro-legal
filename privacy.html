VYRO Studio — Privacy Policy Last updated: 23 October 2026 1. Overview VYRO Studio is a desktop application for creating, editing, and preparing short-form videos for social media. 2. Information processed VYRO Studio may process video files, captions, voice recordings or generated narration, application settings, and publishing queue information that you choose to use within the application. 3. TikTok account authorization If TikTok integration is enabled, VYRO Studio will request authorization through TikTok's official authorization process. VYRO Studio will not ask you to provide your TikTok password directly to the application. 4. Publishing information If you authorize publishing features, the application may process the account information and video content needed to prepare or submit posts. The application should report publishing success only after receiving confirmation from the platform. 5. Storage and sharing Video files and publishing queue data may be stored locally on your device. Information sent to TikTok or other services is subject to their respective privacy policies and terms. 6. Data security VYRO Studio aims to limit access to information and use appropriate safeguards. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. 7. Your choices You can choose which content to create and whether to authorize available social media integrations. You should revoke access through the relevant platform if you no longer want an integration connected. 8. Contact For privacy questions or requests, contact the VYRO Studio operator through the contact details provided with the application. This policy must be updated to reflect the application's actual data handling before public release.
